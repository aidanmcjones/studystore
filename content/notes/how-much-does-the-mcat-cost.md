**Short answer:** The cost of the MCAT is the AAMC exam fee plus whatever you add around it: change fees if your date moves, an international fee if you test abroad, and study materials. The exam fee is set by the AAMC and is public; the study-materials part is your choice and varies widely. This page lists every category so you can see where the money goes. For the current exact figures, use the AAMC fee page and the free [MCAT cost calculator](https://premedtoolkit.com/calculators/mcat-cost/), which adds them up for your situation. This is general information, not financial advice.

## The costs, in one list

| Cost | Who sets it | Where to check the number |
|---|---|---|
| Registration fee | AAMC | [AAMC: MCAT Scheduling Fees](https://students-residents.aamc.org/register-mcat-exam/mcat-scheduling-fees) |
| Reduced fee through the Fee Assistance Program (FAP) | AAMC | AAMC fee page, after FAP approval |
| International fee (testing outside the US, Canada and US Territories) | AAMC | AAMC fee page |
| Reschedule fee | AAMC, depends on days before the exam | AAMC fee page, and our [registration note](mcat-registration-2027-dates-fees-deadlines.html) |
| Cancellation (partial refund, or none close to the exam) | AAMC, depends on days before the exam | Same |
| Study materials: books, question banks, flashcards, practice exams | You and the publisher | Each publisher's own page |
| Travel and a night's stay if your test center is far away | You | Your own plan |
| Retake, if you take the exam again | AAMC | A second registration fee |

## What the AAMC controls

The registration fee, the international fee and the change fees are all published by the AAMC and apply to every test taker on the same date. Because fees are updated between testing years, always read the AAMC page for the year you are booking rather than relying on a number from a forum post or an older article. Our [2027 registration note](mcat-registration-2027-dates-fees-deadlines.html) walks through the 2027 fee tables and the three change windows.

The window you change in matters more than most people expect. Moving your date early is the cheap option; moving it close to the exam costs more and, past a point, the AAMC allows no changes at all. Booking a date with a realistic backup behind it is the simplest way to keep this line item small.

## Fee assistance

The AAMC Fee Assistance Program offers a reduced registration fee and other benefits to people who qualify on financial grounds. Eligibility is decided by the AAMC through an application, so read the program's own page and apply early: approval takes time, and you want the decision before you register.

## What you choose: study materials

Everything outside the AAMC's own fees is a decision, not a requirement. Students spend very different amounts here: some use only free and library resources, others buy several books, a question bank and a prep course. Two ways to keep it proportionate:

- **Match spend to your weak spots.** Take a diagnostic, find the sections that cost you points, and buy for those.
- **Prefer practice you can review.** Questions are only worth the money if you read why each wrong answer is wrong. See [how to review hard MCAT questions](hard-mcat-questions-how-to-review-and-error-log.html).

Free options exist too: this site publishes free [notes](index.html) and a [free sampler of practice questions](../free-mcat-sample-questions.html) so you can test a format before paying for anything.

## Time is a cost too

A longer study period lowers the risk of a retake, which is its own expense, but it also delays your application timeline. How long you need depends on your starting point; see [how long to study for the MCAT](how-long-to-study-for-the-mcat.html) and a [weekly study plan](mcat-study-schedule-weekly-plan.html).

## A simple way to build your budget

1. Open the [MCAT cost calculator](https://premedtoolkit.com/calculators/mcat-cost/) and enter your situation.
2. Check the AAMC fee page for your test year to confirm the fees.
3. Write down your 60-day and 30-day dates the day you register, since those decide what a date change would cost.
4. List the study materials you already own before buying more.

## One-minute recap

MCAT cost has three parts: AAMC fees (public, check the AAMC page for your year), possible change or international fees, and study materials (your choice). Reduced fees exist through the Fee Assistance Program. Use the calculator for a total and the AAMC for the official figures. Choose a date you can keep, and spend on materials where your diagnostic shows weak points.
