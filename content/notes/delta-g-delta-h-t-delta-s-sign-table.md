Gibbs free energy combines enthalpy and entropy: **&Delta;G = &Delta;H - T&Delta;S**, with T in kelvin.

## What the sign of &Delta;G means

- **&Delta;G &lt; 0:** spontaneous (exergonic) in the forward direction.
- **&Delta;G &gt; 0:** not spontaneous (endergonic) as written; the reverse is favored.
- **&Delta;G = 0:** at equilibrium, with no net change.

Spontaneous means thermodynamically favorable. It says nothing about speed, which depends on activation energy.

## Sign table

| &Delta;H | &Delta;S | Spontaneous? |
|---|---|---|
| Negative | Positive | Always |
| Positive | Negative | Never |
| Negative | Negative | Only at low temperature |
| Positive | Positive | Only at high temperature |

Temperature only matters when &Delta;H and &Delta;S have the same sign, because then the two terms compete.

## Worked example

&Delta;H = -40 kJ/mol and &Delta;S = -100 J/(mol K) at 298 K.

- T&Delta;S = 298 &times; (-0.100 kJ) = -29.8 kJ.
- &Delta;G = -40 - (-29.8) = **-10.2 kJ/mol**, spontaneous.
- The crossover temperature is &Delta;H / &Delta;S = -40 / -0.100 = 400 K. The reaction is spontaneous below 400 K.

Convert &Delta;S from J to kJ before combining it with &Delta;H.

## Related equations

- &Delta;G = &Delta;G&deg; + RT ln Q
- &Delta;G&deg; = -RT ln K

## Common traps

- **Using Celsius for T.**
- **Mixing J and kJ.**
- **Reading positive &Delta;G as "never happens".** It can be driven by coupling to a negative &Delta;G.

## One-minute recap

- &Delta;G &lt; 0 spontaneous; = 0 equilibrium.
- Same-sign &Delta;H and &Delta;S make temperature decisive.
- Spontaneous does not mean fast.

:::quiz
Q: What does &Delta;G = 0 mean?
A: The system is at equilibrium.
Q: When is a reaction spontaneous at all temperatures?
A: When &Delta;H is negative and &Delta;S is positive.
Q: When is a reaction with positive &Delta;H and positive &Delta;S spontaneous?
A: At high temperature.
Q: What unit must T be in?
A: Kelvin.
Q: Does a negative &Delta;G mean a fast reaction?
A: No, it only says the reaction is favorable, not how fast it runs.
:::
