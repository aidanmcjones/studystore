**One acetyl-CoA enters the citric acid cycle per turn.** One glucose gives two pyruvate, then two acetyl-CoA, so the cycle turns **twice per glucose**.

## Per turn and per glucose

| Product | Per turn (1 acetyl-CoA) | Per glucose (2 turns) |
|---|---|---|
| Acetyl-CoA entering | 1 | 2 |
| NADH | 3 | 6 |
| FADH2 | 1 | 2 |
| GTP (or ATP) | 1 | 2 |
| CO2 | 2 | 4 |

Where each one comes from: NADH at isocitrate dehydrogenase, alpha-ketoglutarate dehydrogenase and malate dehydrogenase; GTP at succinyl-CoA synthetase; FADH2 at succinate dehydrogenase; CO2 at the first two NADH steps.

## Include the steps before the cycle

The pyruvate dehydrogenase complex makes **1 NADH and 1 CO2 per pyruvate**, so 2 NADH and 2 CO2 per glucose, before the cycle starts. That is not part of the cycle itself, so check whether a question says "in the cycle" or "from pyruvate."

| Stage | NADH per glucose | CO2 per glucose |
|---|---|---|
| Pyruvate to acetyl-CoA | 2 | 2 |
| Citric acid cycle | 6 | 4 |

## Common traps

- **Forgetting the doubling.** Per glucose is twice per turn.
- **Counting the cycle's carbon.** The two carbons of acetyl-CoA are not the two carbons released as CO2 in that same turn.
- **Calling the PDH step part of the cycle.** It is the link reaction.
- **Counting GTP as zero ATP.** GTP is energetically equivalent to ATP.

## One-minute recap

- Per turn: 3 NADH, 1 FADH2, 1 GTP, 2 CO2.
- Per glucose: 6, 2, 2, 4.
- Add 2 NADH and 2 CO2 from PDH if the question starts at pyruvate.

:::quiz
Q: How many acetyl-CoA enter the cycle per turn?
A: One.
Q: How many NADH does one turn make?
A: Three.
Q: How many FADH2 do two turns (one glucose) make?
A: Two.
Q: How many CO2 are released per glucose in the cycle itself?
A: Four.
Q: Which enzyme makes GTP in the cycle?
A: Succinyl-CoA synthetase.
:::
