Fig. 1 · Enzyme kinetics, drawn as you scroll

The inhibition table, in ten seconds of scrolling.

01

Michaelis-Menten

v = Vmax[S] / (Km + [S]). Velocity climbs, then flattens as every enzyme is saturated.

02

Read Km and Vmax

Km is the [S] at half of Vmax. Low Km means tight binding.

03

Competitive

Binds free enzyme at the active site. Apparent Km rises; Vmax unchanged. More substrate wins.

04

Noncompetitive

Binds E and ES equally. Vmax falls; Km unchanged.

05

Uncompetitive

Binds only ES. Km and Vmax both fall, so Lineweaver-Burk lines run parallel.

Fig. 1 · Thin lens, drawn as you scroll

Slide the object in. Watch the image flip.

01

Beyond 2F

Object at 3f from a converging lens. 1/f = 1/do + 1/di puts the image at 1.5f: real, inverted and smaller.

02

At 2F

do = 2f gives di = 2f and m = -di/do = -1. Real, inverted, same size.

03

Between F and 2F

The image runs out past 2F and grows: at do = 1.5f, di = 3f and m = -2. This is how a projector works.

04

At F

1/di = 1/f - 1/f = 0. Refracted rays leave parallel, so no image forms.

05

Inside F

Rays diverge; trace them back. At do = 0.5f, di = -f and m = +2: virtual, upright, enlarged. A magnifying glass.

Fig. 1 · Signal detection, drawn as you scroll

Sensitivity and bias are two different dials.

01

Noise and signal

Every trial gives an internal response. Noise alone makes one distribution; signal plus noise makes a second one, shifted right.

02

Sensitivity, d'

d' is the distance between the two means in standard deviations. A stronger signal or a sharper observer pulls the curves apart.

03

Hits and false alarms

The observer says yes past the criterion. Signal area past the line is the hit rate; noise area past it is the false-alarm rate.

04

Liberal criterion

Slide the criterion left: more hits, but more false alarms too. d' does not change.

05

Conservative criterion

Slide it right: fewer false alarms, more misses. Bias moves the line; only sensitivity moves the curves.

Fig. 1 · A CARS passage map, drawn as you scroll

Read for structure. Map it as you go.

01

Main claim

Find the sentence the rest of the passage defends. Here: a mature canopy is infrastructure.

02

Contrast keyword

"But" is a pivot. It turns against the view in the first sentence, and the sentence after it carries the author's real point.

03

Tone word

Evaluative words reveal judgment. "Shortsighted" says the author is critical, not neutral.

04

Evidence

What supports the claim? Cooler shaded blocks and slower stormwater. Note it in a few words; the details stay in the passage.

05

The passage map

Say the main idea in one sentence, with the author's attitude in it. Then answer the questions from the map.
