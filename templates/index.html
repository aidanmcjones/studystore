Michaelis-Menten

v = Vmax[S] / (Km + [S]). Velocity climbs, then flattens as every enzyme is saturated.

02

Read Km and Vmax

Km is the [S] at half of Vmax. Low Km means tight binding.

03

Competitive

Binds free enzyme at the active site. Apparent Km rises; Vmax unchanged. More substrate wins.

04

Noncompetitive

Binds E and ES equally. Vmax falls; Km unchanged.

Uncompetitive

Binds only ES. Km and Vmax both fall, so Lineweaver-Burk lines run parallel.

FAQ

Questions, answered tersely.

How do I import the deck into Anki?

Install Anki (free, from apps.ankiweb.net). Unzip your download, then double-click deck.apkg or choose File, Import. Cards are tagged under StudyStore::MCAT, and re-importing updates the same notes instead of duplicating them.

What is inside a pack?

Each pack covers one MCAT content category: an original Anki deck, an illustrated study guide PDF, a printable cheat sheet PDF, original practice questions with explanations, and a short README. The {{feat:aamc}} pack, for example, has {{feat:cards}} flashcards and {{feat:questions}} questions. See every pack.

Do I need to use Anki?

The cheat sheet works on its own as a PDF. The deck needs Anki, which is free on desktop and Android.

What if it is not for me?

Every pack has a 14-day, no-questions-asked refund. See the refund policy.
