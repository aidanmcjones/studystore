FAQ

How do I import the deck into Anki?

Install Anki (free, from apps.ankiweb.net). Unzip your download, then double-click deck.apkg or choose File, Import. Cards are tagged StudyStore::AAEnzymes::Topic, and re-importing updates the same notes instead of duplicating them.

What is inside Pack 1?

A 120-card Anki deck (77 short answer, 19 cloze, 16 multiple choice, 8 true/false), a 2-page cheat sheet PDF, and a short README. Topics: amino acids, protein structure, folding, enzyme classes, Michaelis-Menten, Lineweaver-Burk, inhibition, allostery, and regulation. Full contents.

Do I need to use Anki?

The cheat sheet works on its own as a PDF. The deck needs Anki, which is free on desktop and Android.
