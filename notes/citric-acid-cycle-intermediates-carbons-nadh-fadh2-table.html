Use this as a one-glance table of the cycle. Citrate and isocitrate each have 6 carbons, and succinate has 4.

The cycle in order

IntermediateCarbonsEnzyme that makes itByproduct
Citrate6Citrate synthaseCoA released
Isocitrate6AconitaseNone
Alpha-ketoglutarate5Isocitrate dehydrogenaseCO2, NADH
Succinyl-CoA4Alpha-ketoglutarate dehydrogenaseCO2, NADH
Succinate4Succinyl-CoA synthetaseGTP (or ATP)
Fumarate4Succinate dehydrogenaseFADH2
Malate4FumaraseNone
Oxaloacetate4Malate dehydrogenaseNADH

Totals per turn

Each turn gives 3 NADH, 1 FADH2, 1 GTP and 2 CO2. Per glucose, the cycle runs twice, so double each number.

Memory aids

  • The two CO2 leave at isocitrate dehydrogenase and alpha-ketoglutarate dehydrogenase, which is why the carbons drop from 6 to 4.
  • Succinate dehydrogenase is the only cycle enzyme embedded in the inner mitochondrial membrane (complex II).

Common traps

  • Giving succinate 5 carbons.
  • Saying FADH2 comes from malate dehydrogenase. It is succinate dehydrogenase.
  • Forgetting the doubling per glucose.

One-minute recap

  • 6, 6, 5, 4, 4, 4, 4, 4 carbons around the loop.
  • CO2 twice, NADH three times, FADH2 once, GTP once.
