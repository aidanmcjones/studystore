Infrared Spectroscopy for the MCAT: Functional Group Peaks Table

By StudyStore · Published 2026-11-09 · 2 min read · 330 words

IR spectroscopy shows which bonds are present. Bonds absorb at characteristic wavenumbers (cm-1). On the MCAT you mainly need a few strong peaks and their shapes.

Peaks to memorize

BondWavenumber (cm-1)Shape and notes
O-H (alcohol)about 3200 to 3600Broad, strong
O-H (carboxylic acid)about 2500 to 3300Very broad, overlaps C-H
N-Habout 3300 to 3500Sharper than O-H; primary amines show two peaks, secondary one
C-H (sp3)just under 3000Alkane C-H
C-H (sp2)just over 3000Alkene and aromatic C-H
C triple bond C or C triple bond Nabout 2100 to 2260Sharp, in a quiet region
C=Oabout 1650 to 1750Strong, sharp, the easiest peak to spot
C=Cabout 1600 to 1680Weaker than C=O

A fast routine

  1. Look near 1700 for a strong C=O.
  2. Look above 3200 for an O-H or N-H.
  3. Combine them: C=O plus a very broad O-H is a carboxylic acid; C=O with no O-H or N-H is a ketone, aldehyde or ester; a broad O-H with no C=O is an alcohol.
  4. Check for an aldehyde C-H near 2700 to 2850 (two small peaks).

Common traps

  • Mixing alcohol and acid O-H. The acid O-H is wider and extends lower.
  • Missing conjugation. A conjugated C=O absorbs at a slightly lower wavenumber.
  • Reading only the fingerprint region. Below about 1500 it is too complex for the MCAT.
  • Expecting an amide to look like a ketone. Amide C=O sits lower, around 1650.

One-minute recap

  • C=O: strong, sharp, near 1700.
  • O-H: broad above 3200; acid is very broad.
  • Triple bonds: near 2100 to 2260.

Check yourself: 5-question mini-quiz

Try each question first, then click it to reveal the answer.

1. Where does a carbonyl C=O absorb?

Near 1700 cm-1, strong and sharp.

2. How does a carboxylic acid O-H differ from an alcohol O-H?

It is much broader and extends lower, about 2500 to 3300.

3. Where do alkyne and nitrile triple bonds absorb?

About 2100 to 2260 cm-1.

4. What distinguishes a primary amine N-H in IR?

Two peaks near 3300 to 3500.

5. Why is the fingerprint region not used on the MCAT?

It is too complex to assign.
