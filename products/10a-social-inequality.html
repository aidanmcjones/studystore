Try it · sample practice questions

Five questions from the pack.

0 of 5 answered

Q1A neighborhood is racially separated, though no law requires it. This is best called:

B. Without a legal mandate the pattern is de facto. De jure requires a law. Hypersegregation requires high scores on several dimensions, and reproduction concerns passing class across generations.

Q2A Lorenz curve that lies close to the line of equality indicates a Gini coefficient that is:

A. Small area between curve and line yields a low Gini, near 0. A high Gini needs a large gap, 0.5 is arbitrary, and the coefficient cannot be negative.

Q3Comparing a daughter's occupation with her mother's measures:

C. Comparisons across generations are intergenerational. Intragenerational compares one person over time, horizontal means no change in class rank, and structural concerns economy-wide change.

Q4Credentials such as degrees are which form of Bourdieu's cultural capital?

D. Formal qualifications are institutionalized cultural capital. Embodied means ingrained manners and dispositions, objectified means cultural goods such as art or books, and symbolic capital is prestige, so a diploma is none of these.

Q5Recent immigrants with better health than native-born peers illustrates:

B. Selective migration often yields healthier arrivals. Weathering and allostatic load describe stress wear, and social causation describes SES effects.
