Five questions from the pack.

0 of 5 answered

Q1Which of the following is a state function?

B. Entropy depends only on the state. Heat and work depend on the path, and the path itself is not a property of a state. The other choices fail because each describes a quantity tied to how the change happened.

Q2During the boiling of water at 1 atm, the heat supplied

B. At the plateau, energy overcomes intermolecular attractions. T and average KE stay constant, and vapor pressure equals the external pressure.

Q3For a spontaneous process, ΔS(system) = -40 J/K. This is possible if

B. The second law needs ΔS(universe) > 0. A system can lose entropy when the surroundings gain more. In an isolated system ΔS(sys) itself must be positive.

Q4A reaction has k = 4.0 x 10^-3 s^-1. The overall order is

B. A unit of s^-1 is the signature of first order, because rate (M/s) = k[A] requires k in s^-1. Zero order would need M/s, second order M^-1 s^-1, and third order M^-2 s^-1.

Q5Which conditions favor the thermodynamic product?

B. Equilibration lets material funnel to the most stable product. Low T, short time, and irreversibility lock in the kinetic product.
