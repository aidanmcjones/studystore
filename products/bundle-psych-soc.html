Sample questions

5 real questions from the packs in this bundle, with answers

Q1 A person at a party follows one conversation yet turns when her name is spoken across the room. This is best explained by:

  • A. an all-or-none early filter based on physical features
  • B. attenuation, which lowers but does not block unattended input
  • C. controlled processing only
  • D. change blindness

Answer: B. Treisman's attenuation allows highly meaningful stimuli such as one's name to pass. An all-or-none filter would block it. The phenomenon is not controlled processing or a failure to notice a change.

Q2 Which event is directly responsible for the repolarization phase of an action potential?

  • A. Opening of voltage-gated Na+ channels
  • B. Opening of voltage-gated K+ channels
  • C. Inactivation of the Na+/K+ ATPase
  • D. Influx of Ca2+ at the soma

Answer: B. K+ efflux returns the membrane toward rest. Na+ channel opening causes the rising phase, the pump is not what stops the spike, and Ca2+ entry matters at the terminal.

Q3 Fechner's law states that sensation grows with the

  • A. Square of stimulus intensity
  • B. Logarithm of stimulus intensity
  • C. Reciprocal of intensity
  • D. Exponent n of the stimulus

Answer: B. Fechner's law is S = k log I. A square or exponent describes Stevens' power law forms, and a reciprocal would decrease sensation as intensity rises.

Q4 A student does not study the night before an exam so that a poor grade can be blamed on lack of preparation instead of ability. This is:

  • A. Self-monitoring
  • B. Self-handicapping
  • C. Effort justification
  • D. Group-serving bias

Answer: B. Creating an obstacle in advance protects the perceived ability from a failure. Self-monitoring is adjusting behavior to the situation, effort justification is rationalizing the cost of a goal, and group-serving bias concerns in-group attributions.

Q5 A skilled typist and a beginner each type in front of coworkers. According to Zajonc's drive theory, what is most likely?

  • A. The typist improves and the beginner worsens
  • B. Both improve because arousal always helps
  • C. Both worsen because evaluation is stressful
  • D. The beginner improves and the typist worsens

Answer: A. Arousal strengthens the dominant response, which is accurate typing for the expert and errors for the beginner. Neither uniform improvement nor uniform decline fits, and reversing the pattern contradicts the theory.
