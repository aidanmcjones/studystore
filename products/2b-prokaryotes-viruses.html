Where is the DNA of a bacterial cell located?

In the nucleoid, an irregular region of the cytoplasm with no surrounding membrane.

Front · Cloze

Prokaryotic ribosomes are [...], made of a [...] and a [...] subunit. Eukaryotic ribosomes are [...].

Back

Prokaryotic ribosomes are 70S, made of a 50S and a 30S subunit. Eukaryotic ribosomes are 80S.

Svedberg units are not additive: 50 + 30 does not equal 70 in S.

Front · Multiple choice

Which feature is present in a bacterial cell?

  1. A. Mitochondria
  2. B. A nuclear envelope
  3. C. Ribosomes in the cytoplasm
  4. D. Membrane-bound Golgi stacks

Back

Which feature is present in a bacterial cell?

C. Ribosomes in the cytoplasm Bacteria have 70S ribosomes but no membrane-bound organelles. Their ETC sits in the plasma membrane.

B. 4 hours is 240 minutes, so n = 240/40 = 6. N = 3.0 x 10^3 x 2^6 = 192,000 = 1.92 x 10^5. Using 5 generations gives 9.6 x 10^4, using 7 gives 3.84 x 10^5, and 1.2 x 10^4 is only 2 generations.

D. Lipid A is the endotoxin portion of LPS. Teichoic acids are in Gram-positive walls, capsules resist phagocytosis, and porins are channels, not resistance genes.

Q3In Griffith's experiment, which injection killed mice and yielded live S bacteria?

C. R cells absorbed DNA from dead S cells and gained the capsule. Heat-killed S alone cannot grow, live R alone is harmless, and heat-killed R is harmless.

Q4Which is a heat-stable component of Gram-negative bacteria that causes fever and septic shock?

B. LPS (lipid A) is heat stable and triggers TLR4. Botulinum toxin and exotoxins are secreted proteins and heat labile, and peptidoglycan does not drive classical endotoxic shock.

Q5Two influenza strains infect the same cell and produce a virus with a novel hemagglutinin that is not recognized by existing antibodies. This process is:

D. Exchange of RNA segments is reassortment, called shift. Drift is gradual point mutation, lysogenic conversion is a phage process, and influenza does not use reverse transcription.

Sample questions

4 real questions from this pack, with answers

Q1 A culture begins log growth with 3.0 x 10^3 cells/mL and a generation time of 40 minutes. How many cells/mL after 4 hours?

  • A. 1.2 x 10^4
  • B. 1.92 x 10^5
  • C. 9.6 x 10^4
  • D. 3.84 x 10^5

Answer: B. 4 hours is 240 minutes, so n = 240/40 = 6. N = 3.0 x 10^3 x 2^6 = 192,000 = 1.92 x 10^5. Using 5 generations gives 9.6 x 10^4, using 7 gives 3.84 x 10^5, and 1.2 x 10^4 is only 2 generations.

Q2 Which statement correctly pairs a structure with its function?

  • A. Capsule: synthesizes peptidoglycan
  • B. Porin: encodes antibiotic resistance
  • C. Teichoic acid: forms the outer membrane
  • D. Lipid A: activates TLR4 and causes fever

Answer: D. Lipid A is the endotoxin portion of LPS. Teichoic acids are in Gram-positive walls, capsules resist phagocytosis, and porins are channels, not resistance genes.

Q3 In Griffith's experiment, which injection killed mice and yielded live S bacteria?

  • A. Heat-killed R cells alone
  • B. Live R cells alone
  • C. Live R and heat-killed S cells
  • D. Live S cells that were heat killed

Answer: C. R cells absorbed DNA from dead S cells and gained the capsule. Heat-killed S alone cannot grow, live R alone is harmless, and heat-killed R is harmless.

Q4 Two influenza strains infect the same cell and produce a virus with a novel hemagglutinin that is not recognized by existing antibodies. This process is:

  • A. Reverse transcription
  • B. Antigenic drift
  • C. Lysogenic conversion
  • D. Antigenic shift

Answer: D. Exchange of RNA segments is reassortment, called shift. Drift is gradual point mutation, lysogenic conversion is a phage process, and influenza does not use reverse transcription.
