Five questions from the pack.

0 of 5 answered

Q1A city funds free public pools to improve fitness. Residents also form friendships and neighborhood groups. The friendships are best described as a:

A. Friendships were not the stated goal and they benefit the community, so they are a latent function. A manifest function is intended, and a dysfunction is harmful.

Q2A student believes teachers think she is weak at math, then performs poorly. Cooley's looking-glass self describes which step?

D. The looking-glass self starts with imagining how we appear and are judged. Test scores are objective, and pride is only one possible feeling.

Q3Which scenario best illustrates intersectionality?

D. Overlapping identities combine into distinct disadvantage. Equal barriers and single-factor explanations contradict the concept.

Q4Durkheim would most likely interpret communal worship as:

C. He saw ritual as creating and renewing collective bonds. False consciousness is Marx, and the other options do not match his argument.

Q5Which person best fits the sick role as Parsons defined it?

C. Parsons required exemption plus effort to recover and help-seeking. Malingering and ignoring treatment violate the obligations, and blame violates a right.
