Sample questions

5 real questions from this pack, with answers

Q1 A city funds free public pools to improve fitness. Residents also form friendships and neighborhood groups. The friendships are best described as a:

  • A. latent function
  • B. manifest function
  • C. manifest dysfunction
  • D. latent dysfunction

Answer: A. Friendships were not the stated goal and they benefit the community, so they are a latent function. A manifest function is intended, and a dysfunction is harmful.

Q2 A student believes teachers think she is weak at math, then performs poorly. Cooley's looking-glass self describes which step?

  • A. Observing her own test scores
  • B. Reading a textbook
  • C. Feeling pride only
  • D. Imagining others' judgment of her

Answer: D. The looking-glass self starts with imagining how we appear and are judged. Test scores are objective, and pride is only one possible feeling.

Q3 Which scenario best illustrates intersectionality?

  • A. All workers face equal barriers regardless of their identities
  • B. Only gender affects pay, whatever a worker's race or class
  • C. Class alone determines every worker's wage and hiring outcomes
  • D. A low-income Latina faces barriers unlike those of male coworkers or white women

Answer: D. Overlapping identities combine into distinct disadvantage. Equal barriers and single-factor explanations contradict the concept.

Q4 Durkheim would most likely interpret communal worship as:

  • A. a source of false consciousness only
  • B. a rational calculation
  • C. reinforcing group solidarity
  • D. a latent dysfunction

Answer: C. He saw ritual as creating and renewing collective bonds. False consciousness is Marx, and the other options do not match his argument.

Q5 Which person best fits the sick role as Parsons defined it?

  • A. A worker who fakes symptoms to avoid going to work
  • B. A person with diabetes who ignores prescribed treatment
  • C. A worker with flu who stays home and follows a doctor's advice
  • D. A person who is blamed by others for causing the illness

Answer: C. Parsons required exemption plus effort to recover and help-seeking. Malingering and ignoring treatment violate the obligations, and blame violates a right.
