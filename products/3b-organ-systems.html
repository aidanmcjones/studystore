Try it · sample practice questions

Five questions from the pack.

0 of 5 answered

Q1A patient with pulmonary fibrosis has a normal alveolar PO2 but a low arterial PO2. What is the best explanation?

B. Fick's law says diffusion falls as membrane thickness increases, so equilibration is incomplete and PaO2 lags behind alveolar PO2. Surfactant loss lowers compliance, dead space affects ventilation not diffusion, and carbonic anhydrase handles CO2.

Q2A patient on warfarin has a prolonged INR. Which clotting factor is NOT reduced?

B. Warfarin blocks vitamin K recycling, lowering the vitamin K-dependent factors II, VII, IX and X. Factor VIII is not vitamin K dependent.

Q3A mouse lacks functional MHC II. Which defect is expected?

D. MHC II presents peptide to CD4 helper cells, which drive B cell class switching and memory. CD8 cells use MHC I, NK cells and complement do not require MHC II.

Q4Which structure senses distal tubular NaCl and regulates the afferent arteriole?

D. The macula densa signals tubuloglomerular feedback and renin release. Podocytes form the filtration slits, vasa recta preserve the gradient, and principal cells respond to ADH and aldosterone.

Q5An organophosphate pesticide inhibits acetylcholinesterase. What happens at the neuromuscular junction?

A. Persistent ACh keeps the end plate depolarized and desensitized. Botulinum toxin blocks release, curare blocks receptors, and dantrolene-type drugs affect SR Ca2+ release.
