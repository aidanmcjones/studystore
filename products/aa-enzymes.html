Amino Acids, Proteins & Enzyme Kinetics

120 original Anki cards and a 2-page printable cheat sheet for MCAT biochemistry prep.

Description

Stop re-reading biochemistry and start recalling it. This pack covers the amino acid, protein, and enzyme topics that come up again and again in MCAT prep. Cards use the format that fits each fact, with terse one or two sentence answers instead of walls of text.

You get side chain classes, one- and three-letter codes, pKa values, charge at pH 7.4, pI calculations, protein structure levels, folding and denaturation, enzyme classes, Michaelis-Menten and Lineweaver-Burk kinetics, all four inhibition types with their effects on Km and Vmax, allostery, cooperativity, and regulation.
