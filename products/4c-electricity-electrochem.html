Try it · sample practice questions

Five questions from the pack.

0 of 5 answered

Q1Two point charges exert a force F on each other. One charge is tripled and the separation is halved. The new force is:

D. Tripling one charge multiplies F by 3, and halving r multiplies it by 4 (inverse square), so F becomes 12F. 6F misses the square on r, 3F/4 puts the factor on the wrong side, and 3F/2 mixes the two errors.

Q2A battery with an open-circuit voltage of 9.0 V delivers 2.0 A with a terminal voltage of 6.0 V. What is its internal resistance?

B. The lost voltage is 9.0 - 6.0 = 3.0 V across r, so r = 3.0 V/2.0 A = 1.5 ohm. 3.0 ohm is the external resistance (6.0/2.0), 4.5 ohm would be 9.0/2.0 (the total resistance), and 0.67 ohm inverts the ratio.

Q3A 2.0 uF capacitor discharges through a 1.0 megohm resistor. About how long does it take for the voltage to fall to half its starting value?

D. tau = RC = 2.0 s and the half-time is tau ln 2 = 0.693 x 2.0 = 1.4 s. 2.0 s is the time to reach 37 percent, 0.50 s inverts tau, and 4.0 s is 2 tau (13.5 percent left).

Q4A 0.20 m wire carrying 4.0 A is perpendicular to a 0.30 T field. The magnetic force on the wire is:

B. F = BIL sin 90 = (0.30)(4.0)(0.20) = 0.24 N. 0.060 N is B x L x 1 (forgetting the current), 0.015 N is a misplaced decimal, and 3.8 N inverts the arithmetic.

Q5Three metals have standard reduction potentials A+/A = +0.50 V, B2+/B = -0.20 V, and C+/C = -1.00 V. Which pair gives a galvanic cell with the largest E0 cell?

D. The largest E0 comes from the most negative anode and the most positive cathode: 0.50 - (-1.00) = 1.50 V with C as the anode and A as the cathode. The 0.70 V and 0.80 V pairs are galvanic but smaller, and the last pair reverses the roles, giving a negative (nonspontaneous) value.
