Five questions from the pack.

0 of 5 answered

Q1Magnesium has isotopes <sup>24</sup>Mg (78.99%, 23.985 u), <sup>25</sup>Mg (10.00%, 24.986 u), and <sup>26</sup>Mg (11.01%, 25.983 u). What is the average atomic mass?

B. The weighted average is 0.7899(23.985) + 0.1000(24.986) + 0.1101(25.983) = 24.31 u. The simple mean of the three masses (24.99) ignores abundance, and 23.99 is just the most abundant isotope.

Q2A 160 mg sample of an isotope with a half-life of 6.0 days is stored for 18 days. How much remains?

B. 18 days is 3 half-lives, so 160 x (1/2)^3 = 20 mg. 53 mg would come from linear decay, 80 mg is only one half-life, and 10 mg is four half-lives.

Q3Which set of quantum numbers (n, l, m<sub>l</sub>, m<sub>s</sub>) describes an allowed electron?

D. For n = 4, l can be 3 and m<sub>l</sub> can be -3, so the last set is allowed. In (3,3,...) l equals n, in (4,2,3,...) m<sub>l</sub> exceeds l, and a spin of 0 is not allowed.

Q4Which atom has the smallest radius?

A. Across period 3, Z<sub>eff</sub> rises while electrons enter the same shell, so radius shrinks and Si is the smallest of Na, Mg, Al, and Si. Na is the largest because it has the lowest nuclear charge.

Q5How many grams of CO<sub>2</sub> form when 22.0 g of propane (C<sub>3</sub>H<sub>8</sub>, 44.1 g/mol) burns completely?

C. 22.0 g is 0.499 mol propane, and each gives 3 CO2, so 1.497 mol x 44.01 = 65.9 g. 22.0 g forgets the factor of 3, 87.8 g uses the water coefficient (4) instead of the CO2 coefficient (3), and 132 g doubles the answer.
