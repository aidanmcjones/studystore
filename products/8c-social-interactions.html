Try it · sample practice questions

Five questions from the pack.

0 of 5 answered

Q1A woman is the youngest child in her family, a pianist, and a first-generation college graduate. Which pair correctly labels two of these statuses?

A. Birth order is assigned, while becoming a pianist or graduate takes effort. Master status depends on dominance in others' eyes, not on being listed first.

Q2How many pairwise ties exist among 7 people?

C. n(n-1)/2 = 7 x 6 / 2 = 21. Fourteen is only n x 2, 28 is the count for 8 people, and 42 forgets to divide n(n-1) by two. Each wrong number comes from a counting slip, not a different formula.

Q3A clerk refuses a form because a box was written in blue ink instead of black, even though the information is complete. This is best described as:

A. Rigid rule following that defeats the purpose is Merton's ritualism. The scenario does not involve a charismatic leader, role change, or tie formation.

Q4A person grows fonder of an unfamiliar song after hearing it several times. This is the:

C. Repeated exposure increasing liking is the mere exposure effect. The halo effect is about attractiveness, matching is about pairing by looks, and reciprocity is liking those who like us.

Q5A person does not help at an accident scene because many others are watching and each assumes someone else will act. This is:

D. The belief that others will act spreads out personal responsibility. Pluralistic ignorance is misreading calm as no emergency.
