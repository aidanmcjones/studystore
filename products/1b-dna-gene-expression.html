Five questions from the pack.

0 of 5 answered

Q1Two DNA duplexes of equal length have melting temperatures of 85 C and 72 C. The higher Tm sample most likely has

D. G-C pairs have three hydrogen bonds and stack more strongly, so more G-C raises Tm. More A-T lowers Tm, nicks do not raise stability, and lower salt would lower Tm by increasing phosphate repulsion.

Q2Why do human chromosomes need many replication origins?

C. At about 50 nt/s, a single origin could not copy hundreds of millions of base pairs in S phase, so many origins fire in parallel. Human chromosomes are linear, and origins fire once per cycle because of licensing.

Q3The template strand of a gene is 3'-TACGTTAA-5'. What is the mRNA?

C. RNA pairs antiparallel with the template and uses U for T: 5'-AUGCAAUU-3'. The first option copies the template, the second contains T, and the last is written in the wrong direction.

Q4A mutant activator keeps its DNA-binding domain but lacks its activation domain. In the cell it would most likely

D. The DNA-binding domain still docks on the enhancer, but without the activation domain it cannot recruit coactivators. It can even compete with the normal activator.

Q5In a Sanger reaction the ddNTP to dNTP ratio is increased tenfold. The fragments will

C. A higher ddNTP fraction terminates chains earlier, producing shorter fragments. Lower ddNTP would give longer fragments, and termination still occurs at multiple positions.
