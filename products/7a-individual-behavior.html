Try it · sample practice questions

Five questions from the pack.

0 of 5 answered

Q1Which event is directly responsible for the repolarization phase of an action potential?

B. K+ efflux returns the membrane toward rest. Na+ channel opening causes the rising phase, the pump is not what stops the spike, and Ca2+ entry matters at the terminal.

Q2Prolonged elevated cortisol is most associated with

A. Hippocampal neurons are sensitive to chronic glucocorticoids, so the hippocampus shrinks and memory suffers. Cortisol suppresses rather than boosts immunity, raises rather than lowers blood glucose, and chronic stress heightens rather than reduces anxiety.

Q3Two children share a genotype that sets a high and a low IQ range. Their different scores in enriched versus deprived homes illustrate

D. Genes set limits and environment determines the position within them. Genetic determinism ignores the environment effect, passive correlation involves parent-supplied environments, and display rules concern emotion expression.

Q4Which theorist used factor analysis to identify 16 source traits?

C. Cattell's 16PF is built on factor analysis. Allport used trait levels, Eysenck three dimensions, Mischel stressed situations.

Q5A patient has 3 months of hallucinations and disorganized speech with declining work function. Diagnosis

B. 1 to 6 months of symptoms fits schizophreniform. Schizophrenia needs 6 months and brief psychotic disorder is under a month. Schizoaffective disorder also needs a concurrent major mood episode, which is not described here.
