Try it · sample practice questions

Five questions from the pack.

0 of 5 answered

Q1A student fails an exam. Which attribution is internal and unstable under Weiner's scheme?

D. Effort is a feature of the person (internal) that can change from week to week (unstable). Lack of ability is internal but stable, exam difficulty is external and stable, and bad luck is external and unstable.

Q2A student does not study the night before an exam so that a poor grade can be blamed on lack of preparation instead of ability. This is:

B. Creating an obstacle in advance protects the perceived ability from a failure. Self-monitoring is adjusting behavior to the situation, effort justification is rationalizing the cost of a goal, and group-serving bias concerns in-group attributions.

Q3In Fiske's stereotype content model, which group is stereotyped as low in warmth and high in competence?

D. Low warmth with high competence produces envy. Pity goes with high warmth and low competence, admiration with high on both, and contempt with low on both.

Q4Teachers are told that certain students are about to make large gains, but the students were chosen at random. At year's end, those students gained more. This is the:

B. Teacher expectations changed teacher behavior, which improved performance, a self-fulfilling prophecy (Rosenthal and Jacobson, 1968). Stereotype lift, halo effect, and mere exposure do not describe expectation-driven performance gains.

Q5A worker who believes that class inequality is fair and that anyone can succeed by working hard and who ignores structural barriers illustrates:

D. System justification is the motivation to view existing arrangements as fair. Class consciousness is awareness of shared class interests, and relative deprivation is feeling worse off than a reference group.
