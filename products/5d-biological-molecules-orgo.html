How is an ester named from its two parts?

Alkyl group on oxygen first, then the acid name with -oate. Example: ethyl ethanoate (ethyl acetate).

Front · Cloze

IUPAC suffixes: carboxylic acid [...], ester [...], amide [...], aldehyde [...].

Back

IUPAC suffixes: carboxylic acid -oic acid, ester -oate, amide -amide, aldehyde -al.

Ketone is -one, alcohol is -ol, amine is -amine, nitrile is -nitrile.

  1. A. CH₃NH₂

  4. D. CH₃CONH₂

Back

Which compound is a secondary amine?

B. (CH₃)₂NH Secondary means two carbons on nitrogen. A is primary, C is tertiary, and D is an amide, not an amine.

Q1What is the IUPAC name of CH3CH2CONH2?

D. The three-carbon chain with a C(=O)NH2 group is a propanamide. Propanamine would be an amine, propanoic acid has COOH, and N-methylethanamide has a CH3 on nitrogen and only two chain carbons.

Q2Cyclohexanone reacts with a secondary amine (pyrrolidine) and acid catalyst, with water removal. The major product is:

A. A secondary amine has only one N-H, so after forming the carbinolamine it cannot make a neutral C=N and instead loses an alpha proton to give an enamine. Imines come from primary amines, an amide needs an acyl derivative, and an acetal needs two alcohols.

Q3Which species is aromatic?

D. Pyrrole is planar and cyclic with six pi electrons, four from the double bonds and two from nitrogen. Cyclobutadiene (4 pi electrons) and the cyclopentadienyl cation (4) are antiaromatic, and cyclooctatetraene is nonplanar and nonaromatic.

Q4A diterpene contains how many isoprene units?

D. Terpenes are named by units: mono 2, sesqui 3, di 4, tri 6. A diterpene has 20 carbons. Two is a monoterpene, three a sesquiterpene, and six a triterpene such as squalene.

Q5Which compound has the highest boiling point at similar molar mass?

A. A carboxylic acid forms strong hydrogen-bonded dimers, giving a higher boiling point than even the alcohol. The aldehyde and ester cannot donate hydrogen bonds, so they boil lower.

Q1 What is the IUPAC name of CH3CH2CONH2?

  • A. Propanamine
  • B. Propanoic acid
  • C. N-methylethanamide
  • D. Propanamide

Answer: D. The three-carbon chain with a C(=O)NH2 group is a propanamide. Propanamine would be an amine, propanoic acid has COOH, and N-methylethanamide has a CH3 on nitrogen and only two chain carbons.

Q2 Cyclohexanone reacts with a secondary amine (pyrrolidine) and acid catalyst, with water removal. The major product is:

  • A. An enamine
  • B. An imine
  • C. An amide
  • D. An acetal

Answer: A. A secondary amine has only one N-H, so after forming the carbinolamine it cannot make a neutral C=N and instead loses an alpha proton to give an enamine. Imines come from primary amines, an amide needs an acyl derivative, and an acetal needs two alcohols.

Q3 Which species is aromatic?

  • A. Cyclobutadiene
  • B. Cyclopentadienyl cation
  • C. Cyclooctatetraene
  • D. Pyrrole

Answer: D. Pyrrole is planar and cyclic with six pi electrons, four from the double bonds and two from nitrogen. Cyclobutadiene (4 pi electrons) and the cyclopentadienyl cation (4) are antiaromatic, and cyclooctatetraene is nonplanar and nonaromatic.

Q4 A diterpene contains how many isoprene units?

  • A. Two
  • B. Three
  • C. Six
  • D. Four

Answer: D. Terpenes are named by units: mono 2, sesqui 3, di 4, tri 6. A diterpene has 20 carbons. Two is a monoterpene, three a sesquiterpene, and six a triterpene such as squalene.

Q5 Which compound has the highest boiling point at similar molar mass?

  • A. Pentanoic acid
  • B. Pentanal
  • C. Pentan-1-ol
  • D. Methyl butanoate
