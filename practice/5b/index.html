Sample: MCAT Stereochemistry Practice Questions

A newly isolated compound is assigned the (R) configuration at its only chiral center by X-ray analysis. Which statement about its optical rotation is justified without further measurement?

  1. It must be dextrorotatory, because R compounds rotate light clockwise
  2. The direction of its rotation cannot be predicted from the R label; it must be measured with a polarimeter
  3. It must be levorotatory, because R corresponds to the L series
  4. It is optically inactive, because a single chiral center cannot rotate light
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. The direction of its rotation cannot be predicted from the R label; it must be measured with a polarimeter

R and S describe absolute configuration from a set of priority rules, while (+) and (-) are experimental observations of how the sample rotates plane-polarized light. There is no general relationship between them, so the sign must be measured. Choices A and C wrongly link the labels, and any pure sample with one chiral center is optically active, so choice D is false.

Sample: MCAT VSEPR, Hybridization and Molecular Geometry Practice Questions

What is the molecular geometry and hybridization of the carbon atom in methane, CH4?

  1. Trigonal planar, sp2
  2. Linear, sp
  3. Tetrahedral, sp3
  4. Bent, sp3
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. Tetrahedral, sp3

Carbon in methane has four bonding groups and no lone pairs, so the geometry is tetrahedral with bond angles of 109.5 degrees. Four groups require four hybrid orbitals, an sp3 set.
