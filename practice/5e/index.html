Sample: MCAT Thermodynamics Practice Questions

Use these average bond energies for a hypothetical gas-phase reaction X2 + Y2 → 2XY: X-X 440 kJ/mol, Y-Y 240 kJ/mol, X-Y 430 kJ/mol. What is the estimated ΔH of the reaction?

  1. −180 kJ/mol
  2. +180 kJ/mol
  3. +250 kJ/mol
  4. −250 kJ/mol
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. −180 kJ/mol

ΔH is bonds broken minus bonds formed: (440 + 240) − 2(430) = 680 − 860 = −180 kJ/mol. Choice B reverses the subtraction, and choices C and D count only one X-Y bond formed even though two form.

Sample: MCAT Le Chatelier's Principle and Equilibrium Constant Practice Questions

For N2(g) + 3 H2(g) <-> 2 NH3(g), what is the effect of increasing the pressure by decreasing the container volume?

  1. The equilibrium shifts toward reactants
  2. The equilibrium shifts toward products
  3. No shift occurs
  4. K increases
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. The equilibrium shifts toward products

The reactant side has 4 moles of gas and the product side has 2. Higher pressure favors the side with fewer gas moles, so the system shifts toward ammonia. Pressure changes alter the position of equilibrium but not the value of K.

Sample: MCAT Chemical Kinetics Practice Questions

A reaction obeys rate = k[X]2[Y]. When [X] = 0.10 M and [Y] = 0.10 M, the initial rate is 2.0 × 10-4 M/s. What is the rate constant?

  1. 0.20 M-2 s-1
  2. 0.020 M-1 s-1
  3. 2.0 × 10-3 M-2 s-1
  4. 20 M-2 s-1
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. 0.20 M-2 s-1

Rearranging, k = rate / ([X]2[Y]) = 2.0 × 10-4 / (0.010 × 0.10) = 0.20. For a third-order rate law, k has units of M-2 s-1 so that k times M3 gives M/s. Choice B forgets to square [X] and gives second-order units, and choices C and D are off by a factor of 100.
