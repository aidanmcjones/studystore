Sample: MCAT Cardiovascular Function and Blood Gas Transport Practice Questions

A patient has an end-diastolic volume of 140 mL, an end-systolic volume of 60 mL and a heart rate of 75 beats per minute. What is the cardiac output?

  1. 4.5 L/min
  2. 8.0 L/min
  3. 10.5 L/min
  4. 6.0 L/min
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. 6.0 L/min

Stroke volume = EDV - ESV = 140 - 60 = 80 mL. Cardiac output = HR x SV = 75 x 80 = 6,000 mL/min = 6.0 L/min. The ejection fraction in this patient is 80/140, about 57 percent, within the normal range.

Sample: MCAT Digestive System Practice Questions: Enzymes, Absorption and Hormones

Which cells of the stomach secrete hydrochloric acid?

  1. Chief cells
  2. Goblet cells
  3. G cells
  4. Parietal cells
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. Parietal cells

Parietal cells secrete HCl, and in humans also intrinsic factor needed for vitamin B12 absorption. Chief cells secrete pepsinogen, and G cells secrete the hormone gastrin.

Sample: MCAT Immune System Practice: 10 Free Questions

A respiratory virus infects airway epithelial cells, and viral proteins are made in their cytoplasm. How are infected epithelial cells recognized and killed by the adaptive immune system?

  1. Viral peptides on MHC class II are recognized by CD4 helper T cells, which lyse the cells
  2. Viral peptides on MHC class I are recognized by CD8 cytotoxic T cells
  3. IgE bound to mast cells recognizes the infected cells
  4. Neutrophils recognize viral peptides on MHC class I
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Viral peptides on MHC class I are recognized by CD8 cytotoxic T cells

Proteins made in the cytosol are broken down by proteasomes, and the peptides are loaded onto MHC class I, which almost every nucleated cell displays. CD8 cytotoxic T cells recognize these complexes and kill the cell with perforin and granzymes. Epithelial cells generally lack MHC class II, helper T cells coordinate rather than lyse, and neutrophils do not read MHC peptides.
