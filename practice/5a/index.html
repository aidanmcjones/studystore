Sample: MCAT Acid-Base, Buffer and Titration Practice Questions

What is the pH of a 0.020 M aqueous solution of sodium hydroxide at 25 C?

  1. 1.70
  2. 10.30
  3. 12.30
  4. 12.70
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 12.30

NaOH is a strong base, so [OH-] = 0.020 M and pOH = -log(0.020) = 1.70. At 25 C pH = 14.00 - 1.70 = 12.30. Choice A is the pOH reported as if it were the pH, and choices B and D do not equal 14.00 - 1.70; check the arithmetic by confirming that pH + pOH = 14.

Sample: MCAT Solubility, Ksp and Common Ion Practice Questions

A sparingly soluble salt with the formula QY2 dissolves as QY2(s) → Q2+ + 2 Y-. Its molar solubility in pure water at 25 C is 3.0 × 10-4 M. What is its Ksp?

  1. 2.7 × 10-11
  2. 5.4 × 10-11
  3. 1.1 × 10-10
  4. 9.0 × 10-8
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 1.1 × 10-10

[Q2+] = s and [Y-] = 2s, so Ksp = s(2s)2 = 4s3 = 4(3.0 × 10-4)3 = 1.08 × 10-10. Choice A is s3 with the factor of 4 forgotten, choice B uses s2 × 2s instead of s × (2s)2, and choice D treats the salt as 1:1 (s2).
