Sample: MCAT Optics Practice: Mirrors and Lenses, 10 Questions

An object is placed 15 cm in front of a converging lens with a focal length of 10 cm. Which describes the image?

  1. 30 cm behind the lens, real, inverted and twice the object's height
  2. 6.0 cm in front of the lens, virtual, upright and reduced
  3. 30 cm in front of the lens, virtual, upright and enlarged
  4. 7.5 cm behind the lens, real, inverted and reduced
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. 30 cm behind the lens, real, inverted and twice the object's height

1/di = 1/10 − 1/15 = 1/30, so di = +30 cm, a real image on the far side of the lens. The magnification is −30/15 = −2, so the image is inverted and twice as tall. An object between f and 2f always gives a real, inverted, enlarged image beyond 2f, which is how a projector works.

Sample: MCAT Refraction, Snell's Law and Total Internal Reflection Practice Questions

Light travels through a material with an index of refraction of 1.5. What is its speed in the material? (c = 3.0 x 10^8 m/s)

  1. 4.5 x 10^8 m/s
  2. 3.0 x 10^8 m/s
  3. 2.0 x 10^8 m/s
  4. 1.5 x 10^8 m/s
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 2.0 x 10^8 m/s

Speed is v = c/n = 3.0 x 10^8 / 1.5 = 2.0 x 10^8 m/s. Light always travels more slowly in a material than in vacuum, so any answer larger than c can be eliminated immediately.

Sample: MCAT Sound Practice Questions: Doppler Effect, Decibels and Standing Waves

A pump produces a sound level of 60 dB at a technician's bench. A second change in the room raises the sound intensity at the bench by a factor of 1000. What is the new sound level?

  1. 63 dB
  2. 90 dB
  3. 120 dB
  4. 180 dB
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 90 dB

Each factor of 10 in intensity adds 10 dB, so a factor of 1000 (three factors of 10) adds 30 dB, giving 90 dB. The 63 dB choice adds log(1000) without the factor of 10, 120 dB doubles the decibel value, and 180 dB multiplies 60 by 3, treating the scale as linear.
