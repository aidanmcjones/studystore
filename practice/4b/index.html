Sample: MCAT Fluids Practice Questions: Pressure, Buoyancy, Bernoulli and Flow

A diver swims at a depth of 15 m in a freshwater lake (density 1000 kg/m3). Atmospheric pressure is 100 kPa and g = 10 m/s2. What is the absolute pressure on the diver?

  1. 100 kPa
  2. 150 kPa
  3. 250 kPa
  4. 300 kPa
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 250 kPa

The gauge pressure is ρgh = 1000 x 10 x 15 = 150,000 Pa = 150 kPa. Absolute pressure adds the atmosphere on top: 100 + 150 = 250 kPa, about 2.5 atm. The 150 kPa choice is only the gauge pressure, a common slip when a question asks for absolute pressure.

Sample: MCAT Gas Laws Practice: 10 Free Questions with Answers

A flexible weather balloon holds 15 L of helium at 280 K and 0.90 atm on the ground. It rises to an altitude where the pressure is 0.30 atm and the temperature is 210 K. Assuming no helium escapes, about what is its new volume?

  1. 3.8 L
  2. 34 L
  3. 45 L
  4. 60 L
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. 34 L

The combined gas law gives V2 = V1 x (P1/P2) x (T2/T1) = 15 x (0.90/0.30) x (210/280) = 15 x 3 x 0.75, about 34 L. The pressure drop triples the volume while the cooling shrinks it by a quarter. The 45 L choice ignores the temperature change, and 60 L inverts the temperature ratio.
