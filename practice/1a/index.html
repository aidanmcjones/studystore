Sample: Amino Acid Titration and pI: 8 Free MCAT Questions

Alanine has pKa values of about 2.3 (carboxyl) and 9.7 (amino). In a solution of alanine at pH 9.7, which description is most accurate?

  1. All molecules are zwitterions with net charge 0
  2. Nearly all molecules are cationic, with net charge +1
  3. About half the molecules are cations and half are zwitterions, so the average net charge is about +0.5
  4. About half the molecules are zwitterions and half are anions, so the average net charge is about -0.5
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D. About half the molecules are zwitterions and half are anions, so the average net charge is about -0.5

At pH 9.7 the carboxyl group (pKa 2.3) is long since deprotonated. The amino group has pH equal to its pKa, so it is half protonated by Henderson-Hasselbalch. Molecules with a protonated amino group are zwitterions (net 0); those with a deprotonated amino group are anions (net -1). A 50:50 mix gives an average charge of -0.5. Choice A describes the pI (about 6.0), not this pH, and choices B and C use the wrong pKa.

Sample: Enzyme Inhibition and Allostery: 8 Free MCAT Questions

Using the data in Q1, which statement correctly describes the Lineweaver-Burk plot for inhibitor Z compared with the uninhibited enzyme?

  1. The lines are parallel
  2. The lines intersect on the y-axis
  3. The lines intersect on the x-axis
  4. The inhibited line has the same slope and the same x-intercept
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. The lines are parallel

For uncompetitive inhibition both Km and Vmax fall by the same factor, so the slope Km/Vmax is unchanged: 2.0/100 = 0.020 and 0.8/40 = 0.020. Equal slopes with different intercepts give parallel lines. Intersection on the y-axis is the competitive pattern and intersection on the x-axis is the noncompetitive pattern.

Sample: MCAT Hemoglobin and Myoglobin Practice Questions

A purified sample of a muscle oxygen storage protein has a single binding site and a P50 of 3 torr. Assuming simple noncooperative binding, what fraction of the protein carries oxygen at a pO2 of 27 torr?

  1. 0.50
  2. 0.75
  3. 0.90
  4. 0.99
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C. 0.90

For one binding site the fraction bound is pO2 / (pO2 + P50) = 27 / (27 + 3) = 0.90. A value of 0.50 applies only when pO2 equals the P50, and 0.99 would need a pO2 roughly 100 times the P50, so those choices misapply the hyperbolic relationship.
