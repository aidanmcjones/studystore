Sample: MCAT Health Disparities and Environmental Justice Practice Questions

In a hospital audit, patients with the same fracture type, the same pain score and the same insurance plan are compared. Patients from one racial group are prescribed pain medication less often than patients from another group. This finding is best described as:

  1. a healthcare disparity.
  2. a biological determinant of health.
  3. social drift.
  4. an environmental justice violation.
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A. a healthcare disparity.

The patients are matched on condition, pain and insurance, yet the treatment they receive differs by group, which is a difference in the quality of care itself: a healthcare disparity. Nothing here involves inherited biology, downward movement in class due to illness, or the siting of environmental hazards.

Sample: MCAT Social Class, Mobility and Poverty Practice Questions

A woman whose father worked on a factory assembly line becomes a corporate lawyer. Which type of social mobility does this best illustrate?

  1. Intragenerational horizontal mobility
  2. Intergenerational vertical mobility
  3. Structural horizontal mobility
  4. Exchange intragenerational mobility
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B. Intergenerational vertical mobility

The comparison is between a parent and a child, so the mobility is intergenerational, and the move from manual labor to a high-prestige profession is a change in rank, so it is vertical (upward). Horizontal mobility keeps a similar rank, and intragenerational mobility tracks one person across their own career.
